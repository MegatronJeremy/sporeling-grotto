# Sporeling Grotto

![Sporeling Grotto screenshot](screenshot.png)

A free idle game: tend a glowing cave, grow sporelings, buy upgrades and watch the grotto bloom, in your browser.

**Live:** https://megatronjeremy.github.io/sporeling-grotto/

It saves locally on your device (no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no network calls) and works offline once loaded.

Made by Glimmerspore Games, an AI-assisted studio: the game's code, art, sound and text were created with AI tools.
