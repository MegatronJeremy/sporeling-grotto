# Sporeling Grotto

A small idle/incremental game: tend a glowing cave, grow sporelings, buy upgrades and watch the grotto bloom. It runs in your browser, saves locally on your device (no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no network calls) and works offline once loaded.

**Play:** https://megatronjeremy.github.io/sporeling-grotto/

Made by Glimmerspore Games, an AI-assisted studio: the game's code, art, sound and text were created with AI tools.
